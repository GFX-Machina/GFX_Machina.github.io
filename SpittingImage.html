AVALON  &  SPITTING IMAGE

Episodes 5 & 6

Britain’s favourite satirical puppet show - resurrected as a podcast run by Prince Harry, and Paddington Bear.

We filled in for the legendary Lee Watson for two episodes - spoofing logos, music video graphics, and perfectly replicating Melania Trump’s terminator vision!

The whole series was struck down in the Paddington Bear legal spat, but Episodes 5 & 6 (along with the others) have been defiantly resurrected at SpittingImageOfficial.com
