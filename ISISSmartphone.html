Full TV Special

With extraordinary access to a smartphone used by three British men who went to fight in Syria: Mobeen Azhar uncovers what made them join ISIS, and what happened to them.

Claire produced all the computer and phone interfaces for this feature-length documentary. Desktop screens, websites, social media pages, email navigation, video players, conference calls, and text messages that appear over footage.

Every interface, mouse move or page scroll you see on screen was purpose-built and animated!

The Doc originally aired on BBC1, but is not currently available on iPlayer. Clips can be found on YouTube.
