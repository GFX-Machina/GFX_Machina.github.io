MONKEY KINGDOM  &  COMEDY CENTRAL

Across all 6 episodes

Comedy panel show hosted by Jason Manford, where two teams of comedians revel in the UK's most popular pastime - complaining!

Claire worked across all 6 episodes of the show, creating a whole bunch of the cutaway graphics that bookend each complaint.

Claire animated the customer complaints to type-on, and the signatures of support bar to tally up (or not if they're rejected!)

First broadcast on Comedy Central, you'll now find the Complaints Department down the hall on Apple TV.
